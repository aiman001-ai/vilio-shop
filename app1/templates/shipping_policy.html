Shipping Policy

Thank you for choosing Vilio. Here is how your orders are packaged, shipped, and delivered to your doorstep.

1. Delivery Timeline

All orders are processed and dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours. Deliveries typically arrive within 3-5 business days for major metropolitan areas, and 5-7 business days for regional and standard postal destinations.

2. Shipping Charges

We offer Free Standard Delivery on all orders above ₹999. For orders below ₹999, a nominal flat shipping fee applies, transparently displayed during checkout before payment confirmation.

3. Real-Time Tracking

Once your parcel has been handed over to our courier partner (Shiprocket / Bluedart / Delhivery), tracking details and live tracking links are automatically communicated via email and SMS.

4. Domestic & International Coverage

Currently, Vilio fulfills domestic shipments across all serviceable pin codes in India. International shipping capabilities are currently in development.
