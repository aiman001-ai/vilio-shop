Returns & Exchanges

Hassle-free 14-day returns and exchanges for complete customer satisfaction.

14-Day Return Window

You can initiate a return or exchange request within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned in their original packaging with all brand tags intact.

Hassle-Free Size Exchanges

If you experience any sizing or fit discrepancies, we provide complimentary reverse pickups and replacement dispatch at zero additional cost.

Prompt Refunds

Once your returned package passes our quality inspection at the warehouse, refunds are initiated within 48 business hours to your original payment method or designated bank account.

Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene reasons, innerwear, socks, and items marked explicitly under Clearance / Final Sale cannot be returned or exchanged.

Need help? To submit a return or exchange, navigate to your My Orders section or email our dedicated support team at support@vilio.shop.
