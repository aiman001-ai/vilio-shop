Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

The vilio.shop Promise: We respect your privacy. Your data is used exclusively for fulfilling your orders and enhancing your shopping experience, not for resale to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

When you make a purchase from vilio.shop, we collect personal details such as your name, shipping address, phone number, and email address. This information is collected solely to ensure that our logistics partners can deliver your package accurately to your doorstep.

2. Payment & Data Security

Your payment security is our top priority. We do not store your credit card details, CVV, or PIN on our servers. All transactions are processed through highly secure and industry-standard payment partners like Razorpay to ensure maximum protection.

3. Usage of Cookies

We use small data files called "Cookies" to improve your experience. These allow the website to remember your login status and keep your selected items safe in the shopping cart so you don't have to re-add them during your session.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We only share your information with trusted service providers necessary for business operations, such as courier companies (e.g., Delhivery, NimbusPost) for shipping and payment processors for verifying transactions.

5. User Rights

As a user of vilio.shop, you have the right to request access to the data we hold or ask for its deletion from our records by reaching out to our support team.
